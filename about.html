About

Built with a hospitality mindset.

Web Design by Niko grew out of years in hospitality, where first impressions, pacing, and the small details all shape how people feel.

The idea

A website should feel easy to step into.

I think about websites the same way I think about service. A visitor should be able to arrive, understand what is offered, and know where to go next without feeling lost or pushed.


The goal is to make the experience feel clear, comfortable, and specific to the business behind it. Not loud for the sake of being loud. Not generic. Just shaped with attention.

What guides the work

Clear pages start with paying attention.

Listen first

Understand the room

The project starts with the business, the people it serves, and what visitors need to understand before they take the next step.

Remove confusion

Make the path clear

Pages should help people know where they are, what matters, and what action makes sense next.

Finish the details

Let the site feel cared for

Small choices in wording, spacing, imagery, and page flow can change how the whole site feels.
