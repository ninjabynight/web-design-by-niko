About

A small studio with a sharp point of view.

Sharkbone Web Design is built on clear decisions, patient understanding, and websites that feel specific to the business behind them.

The idea

Good design should feel true.

I started Sharkbone Web Design for small businesses and new ventures that want a site that doesn’t feel borrowed. A website should convey the tone, priorities, and character of the business it represents.


The process is steady, honest, and collaborative. The details matter, but the pages still have to work: clear structure, useful content, and a clear path anyone can follow.

What guides the work

The process should feel clear from the start.

Understanding

Start with the business

Before choosing a style, I want to understand what the business does, who it serves, and what the site needs to make clear.

Direction

Know what matters

I’ll be straightforward about what works, what feels unclear, and what choices will make the site stronger.

Precision

Details with purpose

Every page should feel considered, useful, and finished with details that give the whole site more weight.

Project inquiry

Ready to talk it through?

Share the business, the problem, or the idea. We’ll sort out the right direction from there.
